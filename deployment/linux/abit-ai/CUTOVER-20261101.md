## Rollback and safety

Do not restart the Dev-02 source against its old database after Linux has accepted writes: this could lose newer chats, automation results or settings. For any rollback, first stop and fence Linux writers and its dedicated tunnel, preserve current Linux data, and reconcile/reverse-migrate any changes before restoring the old Windows service. Keep migration archives, secret material, browser profiles, databases and credentials out of Git.
